How to test

  1. Press the green button. Turn your sound on.
  2. Choose a language on the keypad, or just write in French.
  3. Write what you would say on the phone. On a mobile, the microphone on your keyboard lets you speak instead.
  4. Tap 🎤 and talk. After each answer the line listens again, like a real call. You can also type.
  5. After each answer, tell us if it sent you to the right place.

Answers use your own Claude account, so the first question asks for permission. Opening hours checked .
